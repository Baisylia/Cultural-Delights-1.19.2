### Added

- Added Wafers, baked in the oven from wheat dough and sugar.
- Added Ice Cream, chilled in the vat from milk and sugar with a wafer as the container.
- Added Ginger Beer, fermented in the vat from water, ginger, sugar and a brown mushroom. 
  - Ginger comes from other mods through the `c:crops/ginger` tag, such as Snowy Spirit or Windswept.

### Changed

- Ginger can be used instead of blaze powder in Spicy Curry, and instead of one of the cinnamon in Cola.
